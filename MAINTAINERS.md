# Maintainers

The maintainers review and merge pull requests. They are the members of the [`@phemera-dev/maintainers`](https://github.com/orgs/phemera-dev/teams/maintainers) team:

- [@fjogeleit](https://github.com/fjogeleit)
- [@salzg](https://github.com/salzg)
- [@The127](https://github.com/The127)
- [@trevex](https://github.com/trevex)

Significant changes are decided through an enhancement proposal (TEP). [TEP-0001](https://github.com/phemera-dev/enhancements/blob/main/teps/0001-tep-process/README.md) describes the process.

How someone becomes a maintainer isn't decided yet. It needs its own TEP, tracked in [#35](https://github.com/phemera-dev/phemera/issues/35).
